# Code of Conduct

UltraShot is a small open-source project. Everyone taking part in it, in issues, pull requests,
discussions or anywhere else connected to the project, is expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent. Most of us are hobbyists doing this in our spare time.
- Keep it on topic and keep it welcoming to newcomers, whatever their experience with HDR, Python or
  image formats.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or sustained disruption are not acceptable.
If something feels wrong, open an issue or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. The
maintainer may edit, remove or lock content and block contributors who do not follow these rules.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
