Lossless. Cuts land on keyframes and the frames are copied as recorded: codec, 10-bit, BT.2020, PQ or HLG, mastering display and content light metadata, Dolby Vision and the audio all stay exactly as they were. Every export is read back and checked against its source.

      
Reframed. A crop changes every frame, so this export is cut losslessly first and then encoded once more on the GPU at near-lossless quality. 10-bit, BT.2020, PQ or HLG, the mastering display and content light metadata and the audio are carried across unchanged, and the result is read back and checked.
